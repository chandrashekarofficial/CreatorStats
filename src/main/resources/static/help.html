Getting Started & Analytics

How do I analyze a YouTube channel?

Enter a YouTube channel name, handle, or channel ID into the search field and select Analyze. CreatorStats retrieves available public channel information and presents it through the analytics dashboard.

Do I need a YouTube account?

No. Public channel analysis does not require you to connect a YouTube account.

What information does CreatorStats show?

Depending on what YouTube makes publicly available, CreatorStats can show subscribers, total views, video count, average views, channel details, historical snapshots, video statistics, projections, live subscriber information, and achievements.

Why is some channel information unavailable?

Some YouTube information may be private, unavailable through the YouTube API, temporarily unavailable, or not published by the channel owner.

What does the Channel Grade mean?

The Channel Grade is a CreatorStats-generated indicator based on available public channel statistics. It is an informational score and should not be treated as an official YouTube rating.

What are Daily Channel Metrics?

CreatorStats can store historical public channel snapshots. These snapshots help show how publicly available channel statistics change over time.

Account & Favorites

How do I save a channel to Favorites?

Sign in, open the channel analytics page, and select Add to Favorites.

How do I remove a favorite?

Open the saved channel and select Remove Favorite, or manage your saved channels from Favorites.

How many favorites can I save?

CreatorStats currently allows up to five favorite channels.

How do I manage my account?

Use the Profile menu and select Account to access your account settings.

CreatorStats Analytics Pages

What is Channel Statistics?

This page provides the main overview of a channel, including core statistics and available historical metrics.

What is Video Statistics?

This page focuses on the channel's published videos and their available performance information.

What are Future Projections?

Future Projections use available historical CreatorStats data to estimate possible future trends. Projections are estimates, not guarantees.

What is Live Subscribers?

This page provides a live-style view of subscriber information when the underlying public data is available.

What are Achievements?

Achievements highlight notable milestones or statistics detected from the available channel data.

Troubleshooting

Why can't I find a channel?

Try the channel's exact handle, channel ID, or another spelling. The YouTube API may also temporarily limit or reject searches.

Why aren't my analytics loading?

Refresh the page and try again. If the problem continues, YouTube data may temporarily be unavailable.

Why do statistics sometimes change?

YouTube statistics are dynamic and can change as YouTube updates public channel information.

Something looks incorrect. What should I do?

Refresh the channel statistics first. If the issue remains, contact CreatorStats support and include the channel and the information that appears incorrect.

Privacy & Data

Does CreatorStats access private YouTube information?

Public channel analysis is based on information made available through YouTube's public services and APIs. CreatorStats does not claim access to private channel information through public analysis.

How is my account information handled?

CreatorStats handles account information according to its Privacy Policy. See the Privacy Policy for more details.

How can I request data removal?

Use the CreatorStats contact page to submit a data-removal request.
