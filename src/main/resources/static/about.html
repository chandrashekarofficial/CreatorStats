About CreatorStats

Understand the data behind YouTube creators.

CreatorStats makes public YouTube information easier to explore, understand and turn into useful insights.

Our mission

Make creator analytics simple, useful and accessible.

YouTube produces an enormous amount of information about channels and videos. CreatorStats is being built to make that information easier to understand.

Our goal is to give people a clean way to explore channel statistics, discover useful patterns and understand the numbers behind online creators.

The data

Built around public YouTube information.

CreatorStats uses publicly available YouTube information to present channel and video statistics through a simpler analytics experience.
